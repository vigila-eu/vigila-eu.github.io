your hardware does the watching

A relay you run. A gateway that reads nothing.

iOS apps can't poll your network in the background — so Vigila ships a lightweight Docker relay (Raspberry Pi friendly). It watches around the clock and seals every alert with a key only your iPhone holds. Our gateway just passes the envelope.
